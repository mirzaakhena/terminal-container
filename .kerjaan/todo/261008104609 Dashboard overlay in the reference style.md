## Background
The style reference is more than a 3D picture: floating white cards around
the scene show headline numbers, the selected site's details, the progress of
a shipment, and a table of equipment. That overlay is what makes the scene an
operations tool. For now its numbers can come from made-up sample data; the
working app name is "PortTrack".

## Request
An overlay in the reference's layout and style — header, headline number
cards, a detail panel, a job progress strip, an equipment table and camera
buttons — filled with sample terminal data, that stays readable on top of the
3D scene.

## Done when
- [ ] A header shows the "PortTrack" name, a search box, a site picker, a live clock and a user badge
- [ ] Top-left cards show at least three yard figures (for example containers in yard, trucks on site, crane moves per hour) from sample data
- [ ] A right-hand panel shows details of the selected block or crane, and clicking a block or crane in the scene changes it
- [ ] A bottom strip shows the steps of the current or a sample job as a progress timeline
- [ ] A table lists cranes and trucks with their status
- [ ] Zoom, rotate and home camera buttons sit at the edge of the scene like in the reference
- [ ] Placed side by side with the style reference, colours, rounded corners and spacing look like the same family
